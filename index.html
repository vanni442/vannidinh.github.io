Pizza Sales Analysis

Transformed raw sales data into a star schema and built a four-page dashboard to examine sales trends, product performance and ingredient demand, with recommendations for staffing, menu and inventory planning.

Methods & findings

Used Power Query to transform the data and DAX to develop dashboard measures. The dataset covers $817.86K in revenue across 21.35K orders.

Friday was the busiest day, Classic the top-revenue category, and garlic/tomatoes were associated with high sales volumes. Findings informed proposed staffing, menu and inventory-planning priorities.

03
Banking research · DissertationFeb – Jul 2024

Customer Deposit Decisions at SHB

Designed a questionnaire and collected customer responses through online and offline surveys. Examined factors associated with deposit decisions and proposed recommendations for deposit services and customer acquisition.

Research approach & findings

Analysed 200 valid, self-collected survey responses in SPSS using Cronbach’s Alpha, Exploratory Factor Analysis (EFA), Pearson correlation and multiple linear regression.

Marketing, deposit interest rates and bank reputation were statistically significant factors associated with deposit decisions. This was an undergraduate dissertation using self-collected survey data, separate from the SHB internship.
